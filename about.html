About Me
I've been travelling for as long as I can remember, and as a tactile person I've always loved collecting souvenirs. I have a lot of collections, from trips alone I've collected fans, miniature building replicas, keychains, and now my largest collection: magnets.

Before starting this project, I had a basic understanding of Javascript and HTML. Now I can say I'm pretty confident with HTML and CSS. We'll see what I decide to do with them next.

Design Insights
Favicons:
Each page has its own favicon.
Countries are colour-coded, because why not?
Each country has its own assigned colour. The palette actually comes from another motion graphic project I made, again, because why not.

Like what you see? Here are the colours and their HEX codes!

Each magnet category has a corresponding icon:

Word
Magnets that are simply a word, like the name of a country or city.
Landmark
Magnets showing a specific building, monument, or piece of scenery from that place.
License Plate
Magnets designed like license plates, either real designs from that locations, or ones inspired by them.
Item
Basically any magnet that doesn't fit into the other categories.
