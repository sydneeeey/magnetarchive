2022
Canada & U.S.A.
destination
Canada - Vancouver, BC
Vancouver License Plate
06/10/2022 - 06/13/2022

One of my favourite bands is 5 Seconds of Summer. I had been anticipating this tour, but unfortunately couldn’t go to their Toronto show. Some of my family and friends flew to Vancouver to see the concert with me. We arrived at night due to flight rescheduling issues. The following day, we biked around Stanley Park and explored the surrounding area. Due to high traffic, we were later than expected, but saw 5 Seconds of Summer at the Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre, on the UBC campus. The next day, we did some shopping and then caught our flight home.
destination
U.S.A. - Kailua-Kona, HI
Kona License Plate
07/08/2022

We flew from Toronto to Kona, stopping in San Francisco for an hour between flights. There was a total Rogers shutdown on this day, and we couldn’t contact anyone at home the entire day! After arriving in Kona, we got our rental car and went shopping at Target.

07/09/2022

The following day, we did some shopping on Ali’i Drive, where I purchased this magnet, then had dinner at the Kona Brewing Co.

07/10/2022

We went shopping at the Waikoloa Beach Resort area.
Hawaii Surfboard
07/11/2022

We drove from Kona to Hilo, cutting across the island. Along the way, we went on the Onomea Trail, visited Akaka Falls State Park, Rainbow Falls, and then went to the Liliuokalani Park and Gardens in Hilo.

07/12/2022

We took a day trip to Mauna Kea, Hawaii's highest point of 4,207.3 meters. We drove to the summit just in time for the sunset.

07/13/2022

We drove along the coast, going to Wailuku River State Park and a black sand beach where we saw some turtles, Punalu'u Bake Shop, and then to the Papakolea Beach green sand beach.

07/14/2022 - 07/18/2022

The remainder of our trip was calmer. We went parasailing and relaxed. We flew to Los Angeles on the 18th.
