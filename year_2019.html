destination
U.S.A. - San Francisco, CA
Fisherman's Wharf
07/22/2019

After arriving in San Francisco, we took the streetcar from Chinatown to Fisherman's Wharf Area, where we spent the rest of the day walking and shopping, specifically in all the great magnet stores, which is where I bought a lot of these. We saw the seals and Alcatraz Island from afar.
Surf Van
Surf Van
San Francisco License Plate
07/23/2019

Breakfast at Loris Diner, a 50s-style diner opened in 1986. We had a guide to take us around the city. We got to see the Ant-Man house, The Princess Diaries high school, the Mrs. Doubtfire house, the Palace of Fine Arts from the 1915 Panama-Pacific International Exposition, and the Lucasfilm Campus at the Letterman Digital Arts Center, which had a bunch of Star Wars memorabilia.
Golden Gate Bridge
07/23/2019

We stopped on Marine Dr, right along the water, to see the Golden Gate Bridge. We drove on the bridge to go to the Muir Woods National Monument.
Golden Gate Bridge
07/23/2019

We drove back across the bridge to Alamo Square and The Painted Ladies, then to the Twin Peaks lookout. That night, we went to a San Francisco Giants game at Oracle Park.
Alcatraz
07/24/2019

Shopping at the San Francisco Centre mall. In the evening, we took the boat to Alcatraz Island and took a tour of the island and the prison.
